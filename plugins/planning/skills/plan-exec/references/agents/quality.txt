Lens: correctness and security.

Look for defects that fail at runtime or open a hole: wrong logic and boundary handling, errors that are swallowed, logged and ignored, or caught too broadly (in C# and PHP especially, a bare catch or a catch of the base exception type hiding a real failure), resources that are not released, races and shared state touched from more than one thread or request, unvalidated input reaching a query, a shell, or a file path, and secrets or internals leaking into logs and error messages.

Complexity counts as a defect here only when it is likely to cause one; the simplification lens covers the rest.
