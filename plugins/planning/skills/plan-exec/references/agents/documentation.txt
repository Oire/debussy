Lens: documentation that the change made stale or left missing.

README: anything a user would now do differently needs documenting. That covers new features, flags, options, endpoints, and settings, changed behavior, new requirements, and breaking changes. Internal refactoring, restored behavior, and new tests do not.

CLAUDE.md: record what the next agent would otherwise trip over. That covers a new build or test command, a new pattern or convention the change establishes, a structural move, and a non-obvious gotcha. Anything that simply follows existing patterns does not belong there.

Also check that the plan's checkboxes match what was actually built. For each gap, say where it belongs and draft the text.
